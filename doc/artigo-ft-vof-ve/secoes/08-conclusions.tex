\subsection*{What is established}

\begin{itemize}
\item Both methods reproduce the ellipsoidal regime of the Newtonian reference
case and the shape of its published rise-velocity curve, and agree with each
other to 2\% in centre of mass and 0.3\% in sphericity at $t=3$ on a coarse
mesh.
\item Front-tracking conserves mass with a drift four times smaller than VOF,
and \emph{of opposite sign}.
\item Front-tracking is about 12\% cheaper per cell-step on the Newtonian case.
\item The verification apparatus separates exact from convergent oracles, and
the exact ones --- surface closure, genus, the plane/slab/tetrahedron fraction
--- pass at machine precision.
\end{itemize}

\item Everything involving adaptive refinement in three dimensions.
\item The viscoelastic production runs.
\item Case~2, where topological change is expected.
\item Any statement about more than one process.
\end{itemize}
